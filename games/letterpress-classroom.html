How to Play

  • Two teams take turns making a word from letters on the 5×5 board.
  • Each board tile can only be used once in the same word.
  • When a word is accepted, every tile used becomes that team's colour.
  • You may steal an opponent's tile unless it is protected.
  • A tile is protected when every existing tile directly above, below, left and right belongs to the same team.
  • Protected tiles show a small ◆ symbol and cannot be selected by the opponent.
  • A word may not be played more than once in the same game.
  • The game ends when all 25 tiles are owned. The team with the most tiles wins.

Classroom tip: Keep Teacher Confirmation on so you can approve spelling, plurals, proper nouns, or vocabulary rules for your class.
